# Compact synthetic snapshot

All observations, model fits, account outputs and figures here use independently
invented OHLC. Ticker names are role labels, not market observations.

The snapshot retains all 864 metrics, all 480 paired intervals, training diagnostics,
the frozen protocol, parent-equivalence receipt, three figures, both assets' full
features/channels/targets/probabilities/model snapshots, and six primary methods'
5 bp / zero-cash-yield ledgers, decisions and trades (12 accounts).

Other costs, cash yields and window/pulse account details are regenerated by the
full offline demo, which writes 216 accounts. Those complete metrics are retained
here; the snapshot does not claim to contain every full-run ledger.

Gzip is deterministic (mtime=0). The snapshot manifest records compressed-file
hashes and their decompressed original-run hashes, plus the original full-run
manifest fingerprint. `tools/verify_breakout_run.py` checks both layers and
independently reconstructs all 12 retained accounts.

The bound parent equivalence compares invented parent probabilities and fitted
parameters plus all 24 common-control ledgers. No private parent path, real
parameter, real result or real input is included. A standalone fresh demo can run
without a parent directory and will explicitly record that no saved parent was
compared. See the methods for how to generate both parent and transfer offline.
