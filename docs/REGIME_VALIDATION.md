# Online regime study: local validation record

Validated locally on 2026-11-01, Python 3.12.6 / Windows. The record below covers
source and installed-wheel checks. Published-head cloud checks are available
from the repository's [Actions page](https://github.com/Mingju-Zhang-CBS/systematic-strategy-lab/actions).

## Tests and installation

The complete suite passed **220 tests** in the source environment, including
20 new regime cases. A fresh virtual environment with system-site packages
disabled installed the locked dependencies and the built 0.11.0 wheel. All 220
tests passed again from copied tests outside the source tree, importing the
installed package. `pip check` reported no broken requirements. Matplotlib's
upstream pyparsing deprecation warnings are retained; they did not fail tests.

Known-answer cases compare forward probabilities with exhaustive hidden-state
path enumeration, distinguish continuous filtering from single-row restarts,
check the Kalman first update and EMA steady gain, and verify real-estimator
prefix invariance across refits. Other cases exercise training cutoff, refiltered
initialization, deliberate failure/cash requests, next-close entry, first fees,
drifted rebalances, funding, independent trade reconciliation and missing sessions.

## Invented experiment reproduction

The complete fixed fixture ran in source and installed-wheel environments. Both
generated 288 metric rows, 96 paired interval rows and 72 accounts. All **227 CSV
files were byte-identical**. The 324 successful fitted parameter snapshots were
numerically identical; the reports and both figures were byte-identical too.
One environment-specific physical-core detection warning differed in the first
fit diagnostic; it changed no fitted parameter or numerical result. Warnings
remain in snapshots rather than being silently deleted.

The separate [audit tool](../tools/verify_regime_run.py) does not import the
account engine. It checks recorded file fingerprints and reconstructs balances
from signed trades, fees, calendar-day cash interest and units. All 72 invented
accounts reconciled; the largest observed residual was approximately
`1.91e-9` model dollars. This establishes selected accounting properties, not
market profitability, source availability or actual fills.

The [checked-in snapshot](../reports/regimes-synthetic/SNAPSHOT.md) retains 31
files, with 30 artifact fingerprints in its manifest. Its subset audit reconstructs
the four retained HMM/Kalman base-scenario accounts. Every other method and
scenario remains in the complete metrics table and is regenerated by the CLI.
The snapshot is explicitly smaller than the complete 234-file generated run.

Separate personal real-reference experiments and their source, output, figures,
comparison receipts and audit records remain private. Their figures and derived
performance numbers are not part of this public validation record.
